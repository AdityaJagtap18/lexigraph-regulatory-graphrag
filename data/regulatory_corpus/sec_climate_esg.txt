UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (SEC)
17 CFR PARTS 210, 229, 239, AND 249 — THE ENHANCEMENT AND STANDARDIZATION OF CLIMATE-RELATED DISCLOSURES
SUBPART 229.1500: REGULATION S-K CLIMATE DISCLOSURES FOR PUBLIC REGISTRANTS

Item 1502: Climate-Related Impacts on Strategy, Business Model, and Outlook
1. Public telecommunications conglomerates, electric utilities, and multinational grid operators must disclose any climate-related risks that have materially impacted or are reasonably likely to have a material impact on the registrant's business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition.
2. Energy utilities and telecom operators must evaluate physical risks to transmission towers, cellular base stations, transformer substations, and data centers arising from severe weather events, flooding, and wildfire exposure.

Item 1504: Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions Metrics and Scope Disclosure
1. Large accelerated filers must disclose direct Scope 1 greenhouse gas emissions and indirect Scope 2 emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling consumed in operations.
2. Where material or included in published reduction targets, Scope 3 supply-chain emissions—encompassing outsourced cloud computing nodes, telecommunications hardware manufacturing, and fuel transport—must be reported with quantitative line-item attestations.
3. Attestation reports for Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions must be provided by independent third-party assurance providers adhering to PCAOB or AICPA attestation standards.

Item 1508: Governance and Board Oversight of Climate Risk
1. Registrants must describe the board of directors' oversight of climate-related risks and management's role in assessing and managing material exposures.
2. Registrants operating cross-border critical infrastructure must disclose financial penalties or pending regulatory actions from foreign jurisdictions—including the European Union CSDDD and national environmental agencies—that exceed $1,000,000 or represent more than 1% of consolidated current assets.
